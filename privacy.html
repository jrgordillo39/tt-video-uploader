📌 1. Information We Collect

Our application strictly collects the minimum necessary data to provide the automated video upload service:

Data Type Source Purpose
Telegram User ID Telegram To verify authorized access and link your session.
TikTok Access Token TikTok OAuth To securely upload videos to your account on your behalf.
TikTok Refresh Token TikTok OAuth To renew API access automatically without requiring constant re-logins.
TikTok Username TikTok API To display the connected account status within the bot interface.
Submitted Media (Videos) Telegram To process and upload to TikTok. Deleted immediately after transfer.

🚫 2. What We DO NOT Collect

  • TikTok or Telegram passwords (authentication is token-based).
  • Browsing data, cookies, or device history.
  • Personal identifiable information such as your real name, email, or phone number.
  • Video metadata beyond what is strictly required by the TikTok API.
  • Data from other accounts or applications on your device.

💾 3. Data Storage & Security

TikTok OAuth Access Tokens and Refresh Tokens are securely stored encrypted on our servers to maintain your connection.

Media files (videos) sent through the bot are processed temporarily in server memory. They are strictly deleted from our infrastructure immediately after the upload to TikTok is successfully completed. We do not keep, archive, or back up any video copies.

🔐 We implement industry-standard security protocols to protect your authorization tokens and data against unauthorized access.

🤝 4. Third-Party Sharing

The only third-party services that process your data are:

  • TikTok — Receives videos and tokens to publish your content. Subject to the TikTok Privacy Policy.
  • Telegram — Processes messages and files sent through the bot interface. Subject to the Telegram Privacy Policy.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or any other unauthorized third parties.

🗑️ 5. Data Deletion & Revocation

You can permanently delete your data from our servers at any time using the /deleteaccount command within the bot. This will immediately erase your TikTok access and refresh tokens from our database.

To manually revoke our application's access directly from TikTok, navigate to:
TikTok App → Profile → Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions.

🔄 6. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect changes in our service or API requirements. Any updates will be reflected in the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the bot constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
