Quick User Guide

What is this?

It's an automated Telegram bot designed to receive your media files and publish them directly to your connected TikTok profile.

Requirements

  • An active Telegram account.
  • An active TikTok account.

How does it work?

  1. Click the "Start on Telegram" button above to open the bot.
  2. Send the /auth command in the chat to securely authorize your TikTok account.
  3. Once authorized, simply send any compatible video file to the bot. It will be uploaded automatically!

Important Upload Limits

  • File Size: Due to Telegram's bot API restrictions, the maximum video size supported is 50MB.
  • Content: All uploaded videos must comply with TikTok's Community Guidelines.
  • Security: You can revoke the bot's access at any time from your TikTok security settings.
